Privacy Policy

App: Bundel Guro · Last updated: September 15, 2026

This policy explains what information Bundel Guro collects when a merchant installs and uses our app on Shopify, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe. We wrote it in plain language so it's easy to read, whether you're a merchant or part of Shopify's support team.

Who this policy covers

Bundel Guro is an app that runs inside a merchant's Shopify store. It helps merchants create product bundle discounts, influencer discount codes, and post-purchase offers. This policy covers the data we collect from the store that installs the app. It does not cover other apps or websites, even if they are linked from our app.

Information we collect

When a merchant installs Bundel Guro, we collect and store:

  • Store information — your store's domain name, its plan, and when the app was installed or uninstalled.
  • Account information — the name and email address of the staff member who connects the app to Shopify, provided to us securely by Shopify during login.
  • Content you create in the app — the bundles, discount settings, influencer names and codes, and post-purchase offers you set up. This may include product names, images, and prices pulled from your store's catalog.
  • Order details needed for post-purchase offers — when a shopper reaches our post-purchase offer screen, Shopify securely tells us the order total, currency, which products were bought, and the shipping country. We use this only to decide which offer to show and to apply it correctly.
  • Usage counts — how many times a discount code or bundle offer was used, so you can see how well it's performing.
What we do not collect:

We do not collect a shopper's name, email address, phone number, home address, or any payment or card details. Shopify handles checkout and payment directly, and that information never passes through our app.

How we use this information

  • To run the features you set up — creating discounts, showing bundle widgets on your storefront, and running post-purchase offers at checkout.
  • To show you accurate reports, like how many times a code was used.
  • To provide customer support if you contact us with a question or a problem.
  • To keep the app secure and working correctly.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

How we store and protect your information

  • All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Your store's access token, which lets our app talk to Shopify on your behalf, is stored securely and is never shared outside of running the app's features.
  • Our app and database run on secured cloud servers with restricted access.

Who we share information with

We share information only with Shopify, because our app works through Shopify's platform and APIs. We do not sell, rent, or share your store's information with advertisers or other third parties.

How long we keep your information

While the app is installed, we keep the information described above so the app can work correctly. If you uninstall the app, we immediately stop accessing your store and delete your login session right away. Other saved settings, like your bundles and influencer codes, may stay in our system for a period in case you reinstall the app. You can ask us to permanently delete all of your store's data at any time — see "Contact us" below.

Your rights

You can ask us, at any time, to:

  • Show you a copy of the information we hold about your store.
  • Correct any information that is wrong.
  • Delete your store's information from our systems.

To make any of these requests, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

Bundel Guro is a business tool for Shopify merchants. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add a new feature that changes what information we collect. If we make an important change, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
